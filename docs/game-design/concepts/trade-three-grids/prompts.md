## 1

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate a polished landscape 16:9 full-screen PC fantasy trading game UI concept for 异世界旅商, 1672x941 or similar. This is a constrained layout refinement, NOT a generic shop screen. Cozy hand-painted anime traveling merchant wagon interior, warm wood and ivory canvas, silver-haired adult elf female customer in green cloak standing OUTSIDE a service opening, village beyond. Critically preserve a three-plane spatial inventory interaction: (1) customer goods sit on the service-window sill at top-middle, (2) a single continuous shallow merchant countertop staging area immediately below that at mid-screen, (3) the player's deep open storage cupboard immediately BELOW that counter, embedded into wagon cabinetry at bottom. All THREE grids visible at once and unobstructed. Player drags physical inventory objects between these three rectangular surfaces. No fourth grid, NO upper-left display cabinet or shelf. Leave that area as simple wagon wall with small pinned route map or canvas. Leftmost narrow wagon exit and rightmost compact bedroll can frame scene but must not consume working area.
Grid specification from provided HTML: customer 24 columns x 4 rows, counter 43 columns x 4 rows, player cabinet 24 columns x10 rows. Keep dense continuous fine square grid, accurate repeated spacing, NOT large rounded independent cards, not 6x5 casual slots, not a list, not a split book. Customer and counter are shallow horizontal grid strips; player cabinet is a deeper rectangular grid, NOT a tall vertical side panel. Use faint cream lines on contrasting cloth surfaces and medium-small multi-cell items (sword 1x4 or 1x5, potion1x2, herb1x2, ore2x2, bag3x3), considerable empty space. Target grid footprints and physical hierarchy matter more than decorative props. All rectangular grid UI FLAT to viewer and axis-aligned, no perspective distortion to grid lines, furniture can have slight depth underneath the true rectangular interactive surfaces.
Minimal quiet Chinese labels 顾客台, 交易柜台, 玩家橱柜 at frame edges, not overlapping cells. One herb selected on counter. Compact legible fixed bargaining panel outside grids: 月光草 ×1 / 估值18G / 对方15G / 还价 −17G+ / 提出还价 / 接受15G / 撤回. At top small diegetic hanging status 第01天 · 风栖镇 and 387G. Customer small speech note “想收一些新鲜的月光草。” and 耐心 ●●●. No top nav dashboard, no huge portrait on left, no giant foreground book, no outdoor rug, no additional showcase. Strong original caravan identity using canvas, folding joinery, travel tags and fabric instead of elaborate dark wood shop room. Clear practical hierarchy, use screen space efficiently. This is a concept screenshot with readable Chinese labels, not a design presentation board.
VARIANT 1: 共轴三层台. Symmetrical central working axis around x=52% screen. Window is broad, x30%-74%, y5%-39%; customer face near x52%,y19%, body ends at sill. Customer grid x32%-72%, y39%-51%. Counter grid x15%-87%,y55%-67%, one uninterrupted long strip with NO decorative objects occupying its cells. Player grid x32%-72%,y69%-99%, directly below. Keep narrow clear join between planes, no large empty walls. Customer and player cabinet match width, long counter extends to each side, creating a readable capital-I composition. Storage front lined with muted teal felt, counter pale linen, sill gray-green cloth. Right upper wall x77%-98%, y12%-49% carries the compact bargaining note, NEVER covers counter. Left upper wall plain pale canvas with small route card and hanging coat but no goods storage. Straight rectilinear carpentry with pale oak and sage painted supports, daylight, welcoming airy atmosphere.
