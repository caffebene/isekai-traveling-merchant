# 玩家主世界画风提示词

全局风格只由 [整体视觉与UI规范](整体视觉与UI规范.md) 定义。本文件补充场景和人物生成约束，不定义另一套 UI。

## 当前方向

复古都市 × 异世界旅商。都市商车、工业加工设备、奇幻顾客与自然材料共存。采用清晰的日式动画画法；街区有生活感，森林有自然环境色。参考《绝区零》的色块和轮廓，不生成其角色、商标或完整界面。

## 正面提示词

```text
Clean Japanese anime game environment, large deliberate graphic color blocks, crisp readable silhouettes, stable selective linework, one base tone with grouped shadows and a few restrained highlights per material. Retro urban otherworld merchant setting. Graphite metal, warm ivory interior and restrained orange markings for the wagon; lived-in daylight urban street with graphic signage for cities; natural grouped blue-green forest masses for exploration. Concentrate details on structural identity and leave interaction areas quiet. Warm light, cool coherent shadows, no surface noise. Environment only, no characters, no UI or baked grids, no text or logos.
```

人物追加：same recognizable identity, costume, silhouette and pose; simplified clean animation shading, sparse internal folds, genuine transparent RGBA, full subject inside canvas, no floor or cast shadow.

## 负面提示词

pixel art, photorealistic, realistic photography, dirty texture, grain, stippling, tiny repeated highlights, realistic cracks or rust, over-detailed surface, excessive neon, bloom, painterly texture, watercolor, sketch, blur, depth of field, fisheye, distorted geometry, readable brands, watermark, baked UI.

## 构图约束

- 当前经营场景保留独立内景、窗外城市、顾客和交互道具分层。
- 柜台保持连续台面；30×12 橱柜定位和自由物理台面由程序定义，不生成柜台格子。
- 门、卧铺、电脑、手机、铃和关门器的轮廓匹配各自点击蒙版；替换时同步校对投放区与碰撞。
- 森林保留独立透明空间层与连续过渡。环境颜色可以变化，UI 控件不得另换材质。

旧概念与历史生成记录只用于追溯，不覆盖当前规范。
